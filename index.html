What We Do

Insulation Services Built for This Climate

Northwest Indiana sees cold winters and humid summers — your insulation has to handle both. Here's what we offer.

Attic Insulation

Blown-in or batt insulation installed to stop heat loss through the roofline and reduce strain on your furnace and AC.

Wall Insulation

Interior and exterior wall insulation solutions that address drafts and cold spots common in older Northwest Indiana homes.

Spray Foam & Blown-In Insulation

The right material for the job — spray foam for tight air sealing, blown-in for even attic and cavity coverage.

Home Energy Efficiency Assessments

We walk your home, identify where you're losing energy, and give you a clear, practical plan before any work begins.

Insulation Removal & Replacement

Old, damaged, or contaminated insulation removed and replaced so your home performs the way it should.

Older Home Insulation Upgrades

Many area homes were built before modern insulation standards. We help bring them up to today's comfort and efficiency levels.
